# Rupak Kumar Soni — Finance Internship Portfolio

A structured portfolio of financial analysis, valuation, investment modelling and M&A work completed during my finance internship.

> **Note:** The workbooks in this repository are the original task files supplied for the internship work. The repository is intended as a portfolio/learning archive. Any company data, assumptions, source links and conclusions should be read in the context of the respective workbook.

## Internship Work — Task Overview

### 1. COGS Analysis — Tata Steel
- Analysed Cost of Goods Sold components across FY2024 and FY2025.
- Examined changes in material costs, stock-in-trade purchases and inventory movements.
- Used variance analysis to understand cost drivers.

### 2. Financial Statement Analysis — Tata Steel
- Analysed the company's financial statements across FY2023–FY2025.
- Examined revenue trends, cost structure and profitability-related movements.
- Built structured financial analysis from reported statements.

### 3. Financial Ratios & Peer Analysis — Tata Steel, JSPL & SAIL
- Calculated and compared liquidity, profitability, solvency, turnover and coverage ratios.
- Benchmarked Tata Steel against JSPL and SAIL.
- Included ratio interpretation and peer comparison.

### 4. Strategic Investment Case — Reliance Industries
- Evaluated multiple strategic investment opportunities.
- Built project-level operating models and cash-flow projections.
- Compared projects using NPV and IRR.
- Incorporated assumptions, scenario analysis and source references.

### 5. Comparable Valuation & DCF — Eternal
- Analysed financial statements and market/enterprise valuation multiples.
- Calculated P/E, P/S, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT and EV/Revenue.
- Built a valuation framework including WACC and DCF analysis.

### 6. M&A Due Diligence & Synergy Assessment
- Evaluated an M&A case involving Mars and Kellanova.
- Structured financial and deal-term analysis.
- Assessed valuation, consideration fairness and potential synergies.
- Documented assumptions and sources.

### 7. EV Charging Investment Model — Reliance / Jio-bp
- Built a five-year EV charging infrastructure investment model.
- Modelled rollout of sites, chargers and BESS.
- Built revenue, operating-cost, EBITDA, capex, depreciation, working-capital and FCF schedules.
- Evaluated project economics using NPV, IRR and payback/valuation analysis.
- Incorporated downside/base/upside assumptions and source-backed market inputs.

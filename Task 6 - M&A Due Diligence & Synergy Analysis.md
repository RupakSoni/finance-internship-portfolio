# M&A Due Diligence & Synergy Analysis

## Objective

Analysed a potential merger and acquisition (M&A) opportunity through financial due diligence and synergy analysis to evaluate the strategic and financial implications of the transaction.

## Analysis Performed

- Reviewed key financial information of the companies involved.
- Analysed historical financial performance.
- Evaluated key revenue and cost drivers.
- Identified potential financial and operational risks.
- Analysed potential synergies arising from the transaction.
- Evaluated the potential impact of synergies on the combined business.
- Used Excel-based analysis to support the transaction assessment.

## M&A Due Diligence

The analysis considered key areas relevant to evaluating an acquisition, including:

- Revenue performance
- Profitability
- Operating costs
- Financial position
- Cash flows
- Key business drivers
- Potential risks
- Transaction considerations

## Synergy Analysis

Synergies represent the additional value that may be created when two businesses operate together.

Potential synergy categories analysed include:

### Revenue Synergies

Potential additional revenue generated through:

- Cross-selling
- Expanded customer reach
- Product or market expansion

### Cost Synergies

Potential cost savings resulting from:

- Operational efficiencies
- Elimination of overlapping functions
- Procurement efficiencies
- Shared infrastructure and resources

## Financial Impact

The analysis evaluates how identified synergies could affect:

- Revenue
- Operating costs
- EBITDA
- Cash flows
- Overall transaction economics

# Tata Steel — COGS Analysis

## Objective

Analysed Tata Steel's Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) for FY2024 and FY2025 to understand the movement in major cost components and identify the key drivers behind the change in total COGS.

## Analysis Performed

- Analysed major components of Cost of Goods Sold.
- Compared FY2024 and FY2025 costs.
- Calculated year-on-year changes in ₹ crore and percentage terms.
- Identified the key drivers behind the movement in COGS.
- Tracked quarterly iron ore and coking coal price movements during FY2025.
- Assessed the relevance of Tata Steel's captive iron ore mining advantage.

## Key Financial Analysis

| COGS Component | FY2024 (₹ Cr) | FY2025 (₹ Cr) | Change (₹ Cr) | Change (%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cost of Materials Consumed | 48,516.26 | 44,088.93 | -4,427.33 | -9.13% |
| Purchases of Stock-in-Trade | 9,699.77 | 9,825.50 | +125.73 | +1.30% |
| Changes in Inventories (FG/WIP) | 379.91 | 330.66 | -49.25 | -12.96% |
| **Total COGS** | **58,595.94** | **54,245.09** | **-4,350.85** | **-7.43%** |

## Key Drivers Identified

### 1. Global Input Price Movement

The analysis identifies the decline in global input prices, particularly iron ore and coking coal, as the largest driver of the reduction in COGS.

### 2. Captive Iron Ore Mining Advantage

Tata Steel's captive iron ore mining operations reduce its dependence on purchasing iron ore from the external market.

## Commodity Price Tracker

The analysis tracked quarterly FY2025 price movements:

- **Iron Ore:** $115–130/tonne in Q1, declining to $95–100/tonne in Q4.
- **Coking Coal:** $200–205/tonne in Q1, declining to $170–180/tonne in Q4.
- Both commodities showed declining trends through most of FY2025 before stabilising in Q4.

## Key Takeaway

Total COGS declined from **₹58,595.94 crore in FY2024 to ₹54,245.09 crore in FY2025**, a reduction of approximately **7.43%**. The analysis links the movement primarily to lower input prices and Tata Steel's captive iron ore mining advantage.

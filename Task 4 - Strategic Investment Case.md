# Strategic Investment Case

## Objective

Evaluated a strategic investment opportunity using financial analysis and investment decision-making techniques to assess future growth prospects, expected returns and overall investment attractiveness.

## Analysis Performed

- Analysed the investment opportunity and underlying business.
- Evaluated historical financial performance.
- Assessed future growth prospects.
- Developed financial projections.
- Calculated investment returns using NPV and IRR.
- Evaluated key financial and business assumptions.
- Identified major investment risks and opportunities.
- Used Excel-based financial modelling to support the investment assessment.

## Financial Evaluation

### Net Present Value (NPV)

NPV was used to assess the present value of expected future cash flows after accounting for the required rate of return.

The analysis considered projected cash flows and the applicable discount rate to evaluate the economic value created by the investment.

### Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

IRR was used to estimate the expected annualised return generated by the investment based on the projected cash flows.

Together, NPV and IRR provided complementary measures for evaluating the investment opportunity.

## Investment Assessment

The analysis considered:

- Revenue growth
- Profitability
- Cash-flow generation
- Investment requirements
- Expected returns
- Growth assumptions
- Financial risks
- Strategic considerations

## Key Financial Concepts Used

- Financial Modelling
- NPV
- IRR
- Cash Flow Analysis
- Investment Return Analysis
- Forecasting
- Scenario Analysis
- Risk Assessment

## Decision Framework

The investment was evaluated by comparing the expected financial returns with the associated investment requirements, assumptions and risks.

The analysis was designed to support a structured investment decision rather than relying solely on historical financial performance.

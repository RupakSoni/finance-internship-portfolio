# EV Charging Investment Model

## Objective

Developed an Excel-based investment model to evaluate the financial attractiveness of an electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure opportunity.

## Analysis Performed

- Assessed the EV charging business opportunity.
- Developed financial assumptions for the investment model.
- Estimated revenues and operating costs.
- Projected future cash flows.
- Analysed the initial investment requirement.
- Evaluated project profitability and investment returns.
- Used financial modelling to assess the long-term viability of the opportunity.

## Business Model Analysis

The model considers the economics of an EV charging infrastructure business, including:

- Charging demand
- Revenue generation
- Operating expenses
- Initial capital expenditure
- Infrastructure requirements
- Cash-flow generation
- Investment returns

## Financial Modelling

The investment model was structured to estimate:

- Initial investment
- Revenue projections
- Operating expenses
- EBITDA
- Cash flows
- Project returns
- Payback / investment recovery

## Investment Evaluation

The project was evaluated using financial metrics and projected cash flows to understand whether the expected future returns justify the initial investment and operating requirements.

Key factors considered include:

- Revenue growth
- Utilisation
- Operating costs
- Capital expenditure
- Cash-flow generation
- Investment returns
- Key business assumptions

# Financial Ratios & Peer Analysis

## Objective

Analysed key financial ratios and compared the company's financial performance with selected industry peers to evaluate profitability, efficiency, liquidity, leverage and valuation characteristics.

## Analysis Performed

- Calculated and analysed key financial ratios.
- Evaluated profitability and operating performance.
- Analysed liquidity and leverage.
- Compared financial performance across selected peers.
- Examined relative valuation metrics.
- Identified key differences between the company and its peers.

## Ratio Categories

### Profitability Ratios

Analysed profitability indicators to understand how effectively the company converts revenue and capital into profits.

Key ratios include:

- Gross Margin
- EBITDA Margin
- EBIT Margin
- Net Profit Margin
- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)

### Liquidity Ratios

Evaluated the company's ability to meet its short-term obligations.

Key ratios include:

- Current Ratio
- Quick Ratio

### Leverage Ratios

Analysed the company's dependence on debt and its financial risk profile.

Key ratios include:

- Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- Debt-to-EBITDA
- Interest Coverage Ratio

### Efficiency Ratios

Evaluated how efficiently the company uses its assets and working capital.

Key ratios include:

- Asset Turnover
- Inventory Turnover
- Receivables Turnover

## Peer Analysis

The company was compared with selected industry peers using financial and valuation metrics.

The comparison focused on:

- Revenue and profitability
- Operating margins
- Return ratios
- Leverage
- Liquidity
- Relative valuation

## Key Takeaway

Ratio analysis provides a structured way to evaluate a company's financial health and operating performance, while peer analysis helps place those results in the context of the broader industry.
